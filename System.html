FACTORY OBSERVATION REPORT

CLASSIFICATION: SYSTEM
SUBJECT: THE FACTORY
STATUS: ACTIVE

HEE has confirmed only a few things about the Factory.

People enter.

Some return changed, displaying abnormal behavior and physical characteristics. Others do not return.

The following day, money is consistently found inside the Factory.

HEE has been unable to determine where the money comes from, why the Factory takes people, or what causes the changes.

Despite this, upper management has approved the continuation of Factory operations.

Personnel will continue to be sent inside as necessary.

The general public is not to be informed of the Factory's activities. Any public reports concerning the structure are to be dismissed or redirected.

Employees assigned to the Factory are to be informed that their work is part of an experimental research program.

No further information is to be provided unless specifically authorized.

The reasoning behind continued operations is simple:

THE FACTORY PRODUCES RESULTS.

Until those results stop, operations will continue.

The Factory takes people.

The Factory changes people.

The Factory produces money.

Everything else remains unknown.
